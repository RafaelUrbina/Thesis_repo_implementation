## Classification Criteria Checklist

A patent will be marked **`1` (Relevant)** if it covers:

* **Core Water Infrastructure & Conveyance:** Pipelines, conduits, aqueducts, distribution networks, water supply, sewage, or wastewater systems.
* **Treatment & Management:** Water cleaning, preservation, or utility management.
* **External Impact / Monitoring Factors:** Monitoring, sensing, predicting, or detecting external, environmental, structural, or physical stressors affecting the water system (e.g., ground movement, leaks, soil moisture, corrosion, weather events, temperature extremes, pressure variations, seismic activity, etc.).

It will be marked **`0` (Irrelevant)** if it falls into:

* **Domestic / Micro-Scale Use:** Faucets, home sinks, drinkware, appliances, garden hoses, or localized plumbing fixtures.
* **Off-Target Industries:** Gas/oil pipelines, pure industrial chemical processes unrelated to water utility networks, or biological/bacterial treatments that don't relate to water infrastructure or external environmental conditions.

---

### Label as **1 (Relevant)**

Any patent related to **water systems**, including:

* Water transportation
* Water distribution
* Water pipelines
* Aqueducts
* Sewage systems
* Wastewater systems
* Stormwater systems
* Irrigation systems
* Water storage
* Water supply networks
* Water treatment plants
* Drinking water systems
* Water purification
* Water treatment chemistry
* Biological treatment
* Filtration
* Desalination
* Sludge treatment
* Pipe manufacturing for water applications
* Hydraulic systems for water
* Fluid mechanics related to water
* Internal process control for water systems
* Monitoring, sensing, prediction, inspection, telemetry, IoT, imaging, leak detection, etc.
* Any external or environmental factors affecting water systems (soil, weather, corrosion, earthquakes, flooding, etc.)

Essentially, **if the invention is genuinely about water, wastewater, sewage, or water infrastructure/management in any meaningful way, it will almost always receive a 1.**

### Label as **0 (Not Relevant)**

Only patents whose primary focus is outside the intended domain, for example:

* Oil or gas pipelines
* Petroleum processing
* Molecular or laboratory chemistry unrelated to water systems
* Household plumbing fixtures (faucets, showers, sinks, toilets, etc.)
* Beverage appliances (coffee makers, water pitchers, bottles, carafes, etc.)
* Refrigerators and dispensers
* Garden hoses
* Hydrants (if solely about the fixture and not water network management)
* Mechanical inventions unrelated to water
* Medical, pharmaceutical, or biological inventions not about water systems
